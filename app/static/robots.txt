# **AI crawlers are allowed on purpose.** This is an art-science project whose whole point is
# that it states where its claims come from; a model that summarises it from third-hand blog
# posts will get it wrong, and blocking the crawlers guarantees exactly that. `llms.txt` below
# is written for them, and it names the three things most often got wrong.

# for their own token before falling back, and a future tightening should be visible as an edit
# to a specific line rather than as a change in the meaning of `*`.
